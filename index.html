💡 Common Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

அடிக்கடி கேட்கப்படும் கேள்விகள் — Transparent answers about borewell drilling costs, depth slabs, casing pipes & sensor water finding

At Anjaneya Borewells, drilling costs follow a transparent Depth Slab System. Standard rates start from ₹90/ft for 0–300 ft, ₹100/ft for 301–400 ft, and scale progressively up to 2200+ ft. Additional costs include heavy ISI PVC 7" casing pipe at ₹400/ft, PVC 10" casing at ₹700/ft, bore bata (₹2000), and optional 18% GST. You can customize exact slab rates and generate a formal estimate instantly using our Live Quotation Calculator.

We operate advanced hydraulic multi-speed rig trucks with high-CFM air compressors capable of drilling up to 2200+ Feet (670+ Meters) deep. Our high-pressure DTH percussion bits easily penetrate hard blue granite rock, volcanic basalt formations, and deep aquifers across Tamil Nadu.

We deploy high-accuracy electromagnetic resonance and electrical resistivity groundwater scanners. These digital sensors map underground subterranean strata, rock fractures, and moisture zones to pinpoint the exact location with highest water yield before commencing drilling, minimizing dry bore risk to zero.

We provide drilling across multiple diameter specifications: 4½", 4¾", 5", 6", 6½", 7", 8", 10", and up to 12" inches. Whether you need a standard 6½" borewell for home/agriculture or high-volume 10"/12" industrial bores, our equipment delivers exact precision.

Old borewell cleaning, mud silt removal, and high-pressure air flushing is priced at ₹40 per foot. Our high-pressure air compressor blasts away years of silt, sand deposits, and bio-fouling blockages to rejuvenate original groundwater flow.

Headquartered in Namakkal, our active operational zones cover: Namakkal (Tiruchengode, Rasipuram, Paramathi Velur, Mohanur, Sendamangalam, Kolli Hills), Salem, Erode, Karur, Tiruchirappalli (Trichy), Coimbatore, Tiruppur, Dindigul, Madurai, and all surrounding regions across Tamil Nadu with 24/7 on-demand mobilization.
